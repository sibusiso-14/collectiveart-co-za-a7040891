# Collaborateart

Project Name: Collaborateart.co.za

Concept: A minimalist, artsy, and modern multi-vendor fashion marketplace where independent fashion designers can showcase and sell their clothing collections.

1. Aesthetic & Visual Style:

Vibe: High-fashion, gallery-like, editorial, clean, and spacious (inspired by digital art magazines and luxury boutiques).

Color Palette: High-contrast minimalism. Primary background off-white (#FAFAFA), pure black text (#000000), and subtle warm grey (#E5E5E5) for thin structural borders. Let high-resolution clothing images provide the color.

Typography: Pair an elegant editorial serif font for titles/headings (like Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display) with a crisp, ultra-clean sans-serif font for navigation, buttons, and body text (like Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).

Layout: Asymmetrical grid, generous whitespace, sharp lines, borderless product cards, and smooth hover state transitions.

2. Site Architecture & Pages Needed:

Homepage:

Hero section: Oversized editorial banner with high-impact visuals and the headline "Where Independent Fashion Meets Art."

Featured Designers Section: Horizontal scrolling cards highlighting independent creatives.

Curated Collections Grid: Asymmetrical masonry layout featuring individual clothing pieces.

"Become a Creator" Call-to-Action section.

Shop / Catalog Page: Filterable by Category (Tops, Outerwear, Accessories), Designer/Brand, and Price.

Designer Profile / Boutique Page: Dedicated visual portfolio page for individual designers with a bio, lookbook gallery, and listed products.

Product Detail Page: Large, multi-angle image gallery on the left; clean sticky buy box on the right with size selection, fabric details, care instructions, and designer attribution.

Designer Portal / Application Landing Page: A clean recruitment page explaining how designers apply, list inventory, and receive payouts.

3. Core Features & Functional Requirements:

Multi-Vendor Architecture: Ability to support multiple independent sellers with separate vendor profiles and inventory tracking.

Checkout & Payout Integration: Integration ready for Stripe Connect or automated commission splits between the platform and the designer.

Mobile Responsiveness: Mobile-first editorial feel with sleek drawer navigation and fast-loading image galleries.


**Live app**: https://collaborateart.co.za
